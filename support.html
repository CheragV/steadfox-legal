Support

PMP Exam Prep, by Steadfox

Write to steadfox.app@gmail.com. Tell us which phone and app version you use and what happened. We aim to reply within a few working days.

Common questions

A question looks wrong. In the app, open the flag on the question and send a short note. Please do not include personal details.

Cancel or manage a subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

Restore a purchase. In the app, open Settings, then Restore purchases, while signed in to the same store account you bought with.

Delete my account and data. In the app, go to Settings, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile and saved answers. You can also email us from the address on your account and we will do it for you.
